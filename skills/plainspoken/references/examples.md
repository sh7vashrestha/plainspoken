# Before and after

The numbers in these examples are made up for illustration. In real text, every number comes from a checked source.

## Email

Before:
> I hope this email finds you well! I wanted to reach out to touch base regarding the project timeline. It's worth noting that we've encountered some challenges that may potentially impact our ability to deliver on schedule.

After:
> Hi Sam, the project will be about two weeks late. The vendor shipped the wrong sensors, and the replacements arrive on 3 March. I can send a revised plan by Friday.

## Product description

Before:
> Our cutting-edge platform empowers teams to seamlessly collaborate, unlocking unprecedented productivity in today's fast-paced digital landscape.

After:
> Teams use the app to share files and comment on them in one place. Most teams set it up in under an hour.

## Paper abstract

Before:
> In this paper, we delve into the pivotal role of edge devices in the ever-evolving realm of AI security. Notably, our novel framework not only enhances robustness but also reduces latency.

After:
> Small devices that run AI models can be attacked through their inputs. We built a filter that runs on the device and blocks 92% of known attacks in our tests. It adds 4 ms per input on a Raspberry Pi 4.

## Cover letter

Before:
> I am thrilled to apply for this role. As a passionate engineer with a proven track record, I believe my unique blend of skills makes me a perfect fit for your dynamic team, and I am eager to leverage my expertise to drive impactful results.

After:
> I am applying for the firmware engineer role. At my last job I moved a toy's audio player from one chip to a cheaper one, which doubled battery life and cut the parts cost by a fifth. Your posting asks for low-power design, and that project was two years of it.

## Essay opening

Before:
> Since the dawn of time, humans have sought to communicate. In today's digital age, remote work has transformed the landscape of modern employment. But is it truly beneficial? This essay will explore both sides.

After:
> Remote work suits people who already know their job and hurts people who are still learning it. I saw both in my first year at a company that closed its office.

## Social post

Before:
> 🚀 Excited to announce our latest paper! It's not just a model, it's a paradigm shift. Huge thanks to my amazing co-authors! 🙏 #AI #Research #Innovation

After:
> Our paper on blocking attacks against small AI devices is out. The filter runs on a Raspberry Pi and adds 4 ms per input. Paper and code are linked below. Thank you to Priya, who ran every experiment twice.

After:

## Leftovers from a template and a chat tool

Before:
> Certainly! Here is a polished version of your email:
> Dear [Hiring Manager's Name], I am writing to apply for the [Position] role at [Company]. Let me know if you would like any changes!

After:
> Dear Dr. Okafor, I am applying for the research assistant role in your lab.

The name and the role came from the user. If they had been missing, the right move was to ask.
